# Protein Powder Domain Knowledge & Guide

## Category Overview
Protein supplements vary widely by source (whey isolate, whey concentrate, plant protein, casein), digestive enzymes, flavor profile, and purity. Beginners frequently experience bloating, taste shock, or digestive discomfort if recommended incorrect formulas.

## Key Terminology & Spec Definitions
1. **Whey Concentrate vs Isolate vs Hydrolysate**:
   - **Whey Concentrate (WPC)**: ~70-80% protein per scoop. Contains small amounts of lactose and fats. Great value, smooth taste, but may cause mild bloating for lactose-sensitive individuals.
   - **Whey Isolate (WPI)**: ~90% protein per scoop. Ultra-filtered to remove almost all lactose and fat. Fast absorbing, easy on stomach, higher price point.
   - **Plant Protein (Pea + Rice / Soy)**: 100% dairy-free, hypoallergenic, higher fiber content. Naturally has a grainier texture and earthy taste.
2. **Digestive Enzymes**:
   - Added enzymes like DigeZyme (Amylase, Protease, Lactase, Lipase, Cellulase) breakdown lactose and complex proteins, preventing gas, heaviness, and stomach cramps.
3. **Protein per Scoop**:
   - Typical range is 20g to 26g per ~30g-35g scoop.
4. **Mixability & Taste**:
   - Instantized powders dissolve with 10-15 seconds of shaker agitation. Non-instantized or raw unflavored powders require a blender and can taste chalky.

## Beginner Buying Rules
- **Rule 1 (First-Time Buyer)**: Recommend whey isolates or enzyme-fortified whey concentrates with high mixability and mild chocolate/vanilla flavors to avoid taste shock.
- **Rule 2 (Stomach Sensitivity / Lactose Intolerance)**: Prioritize Whey Isolate (e.g. Dymatize ISO100, ON Isolate) or Enzyme-Enhanced formulas (MuscleBlaze Biozyme) over plain WPC.
- **Rule 3 (Budget)**: Highlight cost-per-gram of protein. Raw or WPC concentrates (Avvatar, Asitis, Nakpro) offer maximum protein per rupee for budget-conscious buyers.
