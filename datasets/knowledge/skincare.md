# Skincare Domain Knowledge & Guide

## Category Overview
Skincare products on quick-commerce range from gentle daily cleansers to potent active serums (Niacinamide, Vitamin C, Salicylic Acid, Hyaluronic Acid, Retinol). First-time buyers face significant risk of skin barrier damage, purging, or breakout irritation from improper usage.

## Key Terminology & Spec Definitions
1. **Skin Types & Barrier Care**:
   - **Oily / Acne-Prone**: Requires non-comedogenic, oil-free, gel-based formulas (e.g. Salicylic acid, Niacinamide 5-10%, Hyaluronic water gel).
   - **Dry / Sensitive**: Requires ceramides, glycerin, hyaluronic acid, and fragrance-free gentle cleansers (e.g. Cetaphil Gentle Cleanser, CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser).
   - **Combination**: Requires lightweight hydration and mild balancing.
2. **Active Ingredients & Strengths**:
   - **Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)**: Controls sebum, reduces pores and hyperpigmentation. 5% is optimal for beginners; 10% requires tolerance.
   - **Hyaluronic Acid**: Hydrates by binding water to skin. Must be applied to damp skin.
   - **Salicylic Acid (BHA)**: Oil-soluble acid for clearing clogged pores and blackheads.
   - **Ceramides & Hyaluronic Acid**: Rebuilds damaged skin barrier.
3. **Purging vs Breaking Out**:
   - **Purging**: Temporary breakout in areas where you normally get pimples when starting cell-turnover actives (Salicylic, Retinol). Resolves in 2-4 weeks.
   - **Irritation / Breakout**: Redness, stinging, itching, or pimples in new areas caused by comedogenic ingredients or fragrance allergic reaction. Discontinue immediately.

## Beginner Buying Rules
- **Rule 1 (First-Time Buyer)**: Recommend fragrance-free, gentle barrier-building cleansers and light moisturizers (Cetaphil, CeraVe, Minimalist Niacinamide 5%) before introducing high-percentage active serums.
- **Rule 2 (Patch Testing)**: Always highlight patch testing advice on active serums for sensitive skin buyers.
- **Rule 3 (Sunscreen Pairing)**: If buying exfoliants or active serums, advise mandatory pairing with broad-spectrum SPF 50 sunscreen.
