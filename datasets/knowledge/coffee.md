# Coffee Domain Knowledge & Guide

## Category Overview
Coffee choices depend primarily on origin, roast profile, processing method, and brewing technique. For beginners buying coffee on quick-commerce, confusion arises between instant coffee, pre-ground artisanal beans, and dark vs light roasts.

## Key Terminology & Spec Definitions
1. **Instant vs Ground Beans**:
   - **Instant Coffee**: Freeze-dried or spray-dried soluble coffee extract. Dissolves instantly in hot milk or water. Ideal for fast preparation, beginners, and office/dorm use.
   - **Ground Coffee (Filter/Espresso/French Press)**: Requires a brewing tool (South Indian filter, French press, aeropress, or espresso machine). Offers richer aroma and fresh flavor notes but requires 3-5 minutes prep.
2. **Roast Profiles**:
   - **Light Roast**: High acidity, fruity/floral notes, higher caffeine, lighter body. Preferred by connoisseurs, can taste sour to beginners expecting traditional coffee.
   - **Medium Roast**: Balanced acidity and body, caramel/chocolate notes. Most versatile and beginner friendly.
   - **Dark Roast**: Bold, smoky, low acidity, heavy body, bitter finish. Traditional South Indian chicory blends and bold Italian style roasts.
3. **Acidity vs Bitterness**:
   - **Acidity**: Brightness/tanginess (like citrus fruit). High acidity is prized in specialty coffee but disliked by consumers looking for milk-heavy sweet coffee.
   - **Bitterness**: Roasted/charred cocoa finish. High bitterness is common in dark roasted Robusta beans.
4. **Chicory Blends**:
   - Chicory root adds thickness, dark color, and woodiness. Common in classic South Indian filter coffee (e.g. 70:30 or 80:20 blends). Adds body to milk coffees.

## Beginner Buying Rules
- **Rule 1 (First-Time Buyer)**: Recommend 100% Arabica instant coffee or low-acidity medium roast blends for smooth flavor without harsh bitterness.
- **Rule 2 (Preparation Constraint)**: If user has no brewing equipment, avoid pure ground beans unless they own a South Indian filter or French Press.
- **Rule 3 (Flavor Profile)**: Recommend freeze-dried instant (e.g. Davidoff, Blue Tokai Instant, Sleepy Owl) over spray-dried for rich aroma without acidic bite.
